Buy only when the packet is ready

          
Use the paid review when the free precheck gives a qualified reviewer something specific to verify.

          

            SolarPermitPrepAI is for organizing project facts and missing documents before a professional handoff. The
            installer, drafter, engineer, AHJ, utility, and authorized submitter remain responsible for technical and official decisions.
            This is intake and quote prep, not an engineering stamp.
          

        

        

          

            
Good fit for the $49 review

            
    
              
  • You have an address, AHJ, project scope, equipment notes, and a first document list.
    • 
              
  • You need missing-item, document-owner, field-verification, and reviewer-question structure.
    • 
              
  • A qualified contractor, drafter, electrician, engineer, or permit professional will make the final decisions.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Keep the official steps separate

            
    
              
  • Use the packet to prepare a cleaner handoff for plan-set quoting, correction review, or utility questions.
    • 
              
  • Keep portal credentials, signatures, payments, official forms, source files, and private property records outside the planner.
    • 
              
  • Have the responsible reviewer confirm code, structural, electrical, fire-access, utility, and AHJ requirements.
    • 
            

          

          

            
Skip payment when

            
    
              
  • You need an engineering design, PE stamp, permit submission, portal login, fee payment, or utility/AHJ contact.
    • 
              
  • You need code interpretation, licensure verification, inspection readiness, construction authorization, or safety advice.
    • 
              
  • You need guaranteed approval, interconnection, inspection, schedule, quote, payment, ranking, traffic, sales, or revenue.
